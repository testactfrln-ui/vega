Features

One window for the stuff you do all day

Press a hotkey from anywhere in Windows and Vega is there. Type a question or a command and it answers out loud.

A real AI to talk to

Ask anything, get help writing, brainstorm. Pick a faster or smarter brain depending on your PC.

A voice that sounds human

Natural voices in American and British accents, or none at all. It starts talking while it's still thinking.

Timers, reminders, briefing

"Remind me at 3 to call mom." A morning rundown of your weather, reminders, and sites.

Your music

Plays your own MP3 folder on shuffle, or controls Spotify if you connect it.

Runs your PC

Open sites and apps, change volume and brightness, take screenshots, lock or sleep the computer.

Watches your websites

Add your own sites and get an alert when one goes down. Speed, SSL, and Google Analytics if you want them.

A code editor

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript side by side with a live preview, in the style of VS Code.

Notes and files

Quick notes, clipboard history, and a file browser with previews.

Small business tools

An optional tab for jobs, quotes you can copy and send, and a calendar.

Privacy

Nothing you say leaves your computer

Most AI assistants send everything you type to a server. Vega's AI runs on your own PC through Ollama, a free program it helps you install. Your conversations, notes, and settings stay on your machine.

  • No account, no sign-up, no email.
  • No subscription. The AI is free to run forever.
  • Works offline once it's set up (weather and websites need internet, of course).
  • Spotify, Google Analytics, and your websites are only connected if you add them yourself.
Install

Set up in a few minutes

Vega walks you through everything the first time it opens.

Download and run

Run Vega-Assistant-Setup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. That message appears for new apps that aren't from a big publisher.

Name it and pick a voice

Call it Vega, JARVIS, Friday, anything. Choose how it talks and what it calls you.

Get the AI

Vega links you to Ollama (free), then downloads the right AI model for your PC with one click. 1 to 5 GB, depending on the model.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Vega and the AI it runs on are free. There's nothing to subscribe to and nothing to unlock.

Do I need a powerful computer?

Any recent Windows laptop with 8 GB of memory works with the "Fastest" brain. With 16 GB or more, the "Balanced" and "Smartest" brains give better answers. Vega suggests one based on your PC.

Can I call it JARVIS?

Yes. The first time Vega opens you pick its name and what it calls you. You can change both any time in Settings.

Can I talk to it with my voice?

Not yet. You type, and Vega answers out loud. Voice input is on the list.

How do updates work?

Vega checks for updates every few hours, downloads them in the background, and shows a "Restart and update" button. Your settings and data are kept.

Is there a Mac version?

Not right now. Vega is Windows only.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Vega Assistant → Uninstall. To remove the AI too, uninstall Ollama the same way.
